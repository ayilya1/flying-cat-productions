Project Story

Between identities.
Between lives.

Chandrakaal is a five-minute animated short inspired by the story of Ila and Sudyumna, a figure whose existence moves between different identities.

Through mythology and animation, the film explores transformation, gender, and the instability of the categories through which identity is often understood.

Rather than treating transformation as a simple movement from one fixed state to another, Chandrakaal considers identity as something that can be lived, negotiated, and transformed over time.

Ila and Sudyumna

At the centre of the film is the story of Ila and Sudyumna. Their transformation creates a world in which gender is not easily contained within a single, permanent identity.

Chandrakaal interprets this story through animation, using movement, colour, and visual transformation to travel between different states of being.

Looking backward to look forward

The film places a story from South Asian mythology beside contemporary questions of identity and belonging.

The mythology is not treated simply as something belonging to the past, but as a story that continues to carry meaning in the present.
